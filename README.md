# SADA (صَدَى) 📱💎
> An evidence-based, gamified platform designed to assist in at-home speech and language therapy for children (Ages 3+).

## 🚀 About the Project
SADA addresses the critical challenges of inaccessibility, high cost, and low engagement associated with traditional speech-language pathology (SLP) services. It is a **"Parent-Assisted"** application that digitizes clinically-validated therapeutic protocols into engaging, interactive, and generative games to ensure activities remain non-repetitive and highly engaging.

## 🎯 Target Clinical Coverage
The platform targets the primary clusters of speech-language disorders (SLDs) by adapting established clinical protocols:
* **Articulation Disorders** (Van Riper's Hierarchical Approach)
* **Fluency Disorders (Stuttering)**
* **Voice Disorders** (Biofeedback for Voice)
* **Expressive Language Disorders** (Narrative Therapy)
* **Social Pragmatic Disorders**
* **Childhood Apraxia of Speech**

## ✨ Key Features & Ecosystem
* **Interactive & Generative Games:** Keeps children motivated and prevents repetitive fatigue.
* **Parent Dashboard:** Empowers parents to monitor, guide, and track their child’s progress effectively at home.
* **Admin & Clinical Management:** Robust structure for managing therapeutic protocols and user data.
* **Comprehensive Modules:** Includes Registration, Landing Page, Games, Parent Dashboard, Admin Dashboard, and Calm Page.

## ⚙️ Tech Stack & Architecture
* **Backend:** Python, FastAPI
* **Frontend:** Responsive Web Technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
* **AI & NLP:** AI-driven speech analysis and pattern recognition modules.
